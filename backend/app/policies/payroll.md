SAMPLE POLICY - for demonstration only, not a real company policy.

- Salaries are paid on the last working day of each month.
- Payslips are available in the employee portal from the 25th.
- Overtime is paid with the salary of the following month.
- Errors on a payslip are corrected with the next salary payment,
  or within 5 working days if more than 200 EUR is missing.
