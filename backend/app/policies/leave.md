SAMPLE POLICY - for demonstration only, not a real company policy.

- Full-time employees get 30 vacation days per calendar year.
- Vacation requests must be submitted at least 2 weeks in advance.
- Up to 5 unused days can be carried over until 31 March of the next year.
- Sick leave: inform your team lead before your shift starts; a doctor's
  note is required from the 3rd day of illness.
